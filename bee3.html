Interactive Single Phase Induction Motor

Construction

The motor consists of:

  • Stator: Fixed part with winding connected to power supply.
  • Rotor: Squirrel cage type, rotates due to induced EMF.

Working Principle

The motor works on electromagnetic induction. A pulsating magnetic field is produced which interacts with the rotor. Since it’s not self-starting, auxiliary windings (like capacitors or split phase) help start rotation.

Applications

Split Phase Motor

Used in fans, washing machines, small tools.

Capacitor Start Motor

Used in compressors and water pumps.

Capacitor Run Motor

Used in air conditioners and refrigerators.
